These notes and the research are the only facts available about what happened. Any concrete event, result, error, number, timing or worked example (what was built, what broke, what the agent did, what changed after a fix) must come from a note below or from the research, whether it is written as "I", "we", "you" or impersonally. Do not invent an illustrative scenario to fill a section. When a section intent needs such a fact and none is available, write the marker `[EXPERIENCE: what would go here]` on its own line in that spot, and write no sentence about the marker or the missing fact.
