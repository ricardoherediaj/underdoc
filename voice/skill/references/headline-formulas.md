# Headline Formulas (Nicolas Cole + Ricardo Voice)

## The 3-Question Framework

Every headline MUST answer:

1. **WHAT** is this about?
2. **WHO** is this for?
3. **What's the PROMISE?** (outcome to achieve or avoid)

**Weak headlines** answer 1 of 3 questions.
**Good headlines** answer 2 of 3 questions.
**Exceptional headlines** answer all 3 questions.

---

## Headline Anatomy

### The Structure

```
"The [NUMBER] [WHAT] That [ACTION VERB] Every Single [WHO] {PROMISE}"
```

**Example**:
"The 1 Question That Gets Every Single Millennial {In Trouble}"

**Breakdown**:
- **"The 1"**: Declarative tone, low barrier to entry
- **"Question"**: The WHAT (stirs curiosity)
- **"That Gets"**: Connecting piece (strong verb)
- **"Every Single Millennial"**: The WHO (specific targeting)
- **"{In Trouble}"**: The PROMISE (emotional outcome)

## Proven Formats for Ricardo's Content

### 1. Big Number + Technical Outcome

**Formula**: "How I [ACTION] from [BAD NUMBER] to [GOOD NUMBER] ([PERCENTAGE] [METRIC])"

**Examples**:
- "How I Reduced RAG Query Costs from $1 to $0.01 (80% Token Reduction)"
- "From 1800 to 360 Tokens: How Evaluation-Driven Development Cut Prompt Costs"
- "Building a RAG System That Achieves 99.4% Faithfulness (Under $0.50 for 100 Queries)"

**Why it works**: Quantified results prove credibility before reading

### 2. Contrarian Take + Universal Impact

**Formula**: "[TECHNICAL TOPIC] [SURPRISING CLAIM] (And What That Means for [BROADER AUDIENCE])"

**Examples**:
- "RAG Systems Fail at the Data Layer, Not the Model Layer (What This Means for Any AI Project)"
- "Why uv Beats pip by 100x (Even If You're Not a Python Expert)"
- "Evaluation Matters More Than Prompt Engineering (Here's the Data That Proves It)"

**Why it works**: Challenges assumptions, promises new perspective

### 3. Framework/Methodology Introduction

**Formula**: "[METHODOLOGY]: [SPECIFIC OUTCOME] for [WHO]"

**Examples**:
- "Evaluation-Driven Development: Building RAG Systems That Actually Work"
- "The 2-Chunk ChromaDB Architecture That Delivers 100% Retrieval Accuracy"
- "Prompt-First Design: How to Build Vector Databases That Match Your Queries"

**Why it works**: Promises a repeatable system readers can apply

### 4. Journey/Transformation

**Formula**: "From [BAD STATE] to [GOOD STATE]: [HOW/WHY] for [WHO]"

**Examples**:
- "From Vibes-Based to Metric-Driven: How I Learned to Evaluate RAG Outputs"
- "From Medicine to AI Engineering: What the Career Pivot Actually Looks Like"
- "From 'Good Enough' to 99.4% Accurate: Building an LLM Evaluation Framework"

**Why it works**: Relatability (bad state) + aspiration (good state)

### 5. Problem + Solution Statement

**Formula**: "[SPECIFIC PROBLEM] Here's [NUMBER] Ways to [SOLVE IT]"

**Examples**:
- "Your RAG System Hallucinates Statistics. Here's How to Fix It (With Ground Truth Validation)"
- "LLMs Cost Too Much at Scale. Here Are 5 Token Optimization Techniques That Cut Costs 80%"
- "Python Dependencies Are a Mess. Here's Why uv Solves It in 18 Seconds"

**Why it works**: Names the pain point readers feel right now

### 6. Niche Topic + Universal Question

**Formula**: "[TECHNICAL DETAIL] Teaches Us About [UNIVERSAL CONCEPT]"

**Examples**:
- "What the Home/Away Swap Bug Teaches Us About Data Quality in AI Systems"
- "How Football RAG Systems Reveal the Data Engineering Problem in Every AI Project"
- "Why Choosing ChromaDB Over Pinecone Matters for Any Vector Database Decision"

**Why it works**: Bridges technical depth with broader applicability

## POWER Phrases to Add

These amplify specificity and urgency:

### Instead of "Ways..."
- "Little-Known Ways..."
- "Unconventional Ways..."
- "Small But Powerful Ways..."
- "Proven Ways..."

### Instead of "How to..."
- "The Step-by-Step Framework for..."
- "The Practical Guide to..."
- "The Real-World Approach to..."

### For Technical Content
- "Under-the-Hood Look at..."
- "Behind-the-Scenes of..."
- "Deep Dive Into..."
- "Technical Breakdown of..."

### For Career/Journey
- "Lessons Learned..."
- "What [X Years] of [Experience] Taught Me About..."
- "The Uncomfortable Truth About..."

## Ricardo's Honest Style (NOT Hype)

### ✅ Use (Authentic)
- "How I Actually Built..."
- "What [Number] Matches Taught Me..."
- "The Bug That Changed My Entire Approach..."
- "Why I Chose [Tool] Over [Tool] (Honest Trade-offs)"

### ❌ Avoid (Marketing Hype)
- "Revolutionary New Way..."
- "Game-Changing Approach..."
- "Transform Your Workflow Forever..."
- "The Secret Nobody Tells You..."

**Principle**: Under-promise, over-deliver. Let the metrics do the talking.

## Headline Testing Process

### Step 1: Write 10+ Variations

**BuzzFeed rule**: Write 30 headlines to find the right one

**Minimum**: Write 10 variations before choosing

**Method**:
1. Start with working title
2. Apply different formulas
3. Test variations of each
4. Pick top 3
5. Apply POWER phrases
6. Choose final

### Step 2: 3-Question Test

For each candidate:
- ✅ **WHAT**: Is it clear what this is about?
- ✅ **WHO**: Does it identify the target reader?
- ✅ **PROMISE**: Is the outcome/benefit clear?

If any answer is "no", rewrite.

### Step 3: Ricardo Voice Check

- [ ] Honest, not hype-y?
- [ ] Specific (mentions tool/number)?
- [ ] Technically credible?
- [ ] Would I actually say this?

## Common Headline Mistakes

### ❌ Too Clever
"The Cartography of Code: Navigating the Labyrinth of RAG Architecture"

**Problem**: Obscures WHAT/WHO/PROMISE with metaphor

✅ **Fix**: "The RAG Architecture That Delivers 99.4% Accuracy (Technical Breakdown for AI Engineers)"

### ❌ No Specificity
"Better Ways to Build AI Systems"

**Problem**: "Better" is subjective, "Ways" is vague

✅ **Fix**: "5 Data Quality Checks That Prevented 95% of Hallucinations in My RAG System"

### ❌ Wrong Audience Signal
"Machine Learning Paradigms in Contemporary Computational Intelligence Systems"

**Problem**: Academic language signals academic audience (not Ricardo's readers)

✅ **Fix**: "Why I Chose Sentence-Transformers Over OpenAI Embeddings (Trade-offs Explained)"

## Final Headline Checklist

Before publishing:

- [ ] Answers all 3 questions (WHAT/WHO/PROMISE)
- [ ] Includes specific number or metric
- [ ] Mentions tool/framework by name (if technical post)
- [ ] Honest tone (not hype)
- [ ] Under 100 characters (for social sharing)
- [ ] Tested 10+ variations
- [ ] Would I click this?

**Remember**: Headline is a micro-version of the entire post. It must deliver on what it promises.
